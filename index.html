ℹ️ How to Play

  1. Cast your fishing line.
  2. Choose the correct synonym.
  3. Choose the correct meaning of the word.
  4. Complete both questions to earn coins and XP.
  5. Find special fish to collect secret code fragments.
  6. Upgrade your line to improve your special-fish odds.
